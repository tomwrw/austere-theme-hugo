Not everything wants a date on it. A page with no `date` in its front matter
gets no dated header, and in list pages it drops into an **Undated** block
rather than sorting itself to 1 January of year one.

Set `undatedPosition = "above"` in `[params]` to put that block before the
dated entries instead of after them.
